- Dragon Rock's top no longer crashes at its east end, where more of the level is loaded at once than the game kept
  room for.
- Kamerian Dragon on Restored: the Skeetla arena at the bottom of Dragon Rock, once won, leads into the Kamerian
  Dragon's room, which nothing led to, and the fourth SpellStone, once picked up, leads back out to the arena.
- Krazoa Palace on Restored: the palace's four wings each have a door, a console and a text block, played in turn
  from Cape Claw's tablet room, and the last door they open leads to the red Krazoa, who sends the player on to
  Drakor. The prototype built the rooms and left them empty.
- Baby CloudRunners on Restored: Kyte finds CloudRunner Fortress's hidden baby CloudRunners once she is near one, so
  it comes out for Krystal to rescue. In the prototype Kyte never found the ones waiting for her.
- Picking up an item, a key, a crystal, a tooth or a SpellStone no longer makes a second Sabre whose scene never
  ends, and no longer crashes.
- Walled City: standing on either pressure switch no longer crashes, and the sun and moon beacons can be hit, so the
  city's slab door opens.
- BOSS TRex can be beaten: Sabre is no longer shut out of the arena, standing on a floor switch fires its laser wall,
  and the TRex keeps charging and facing its way round the ring after each hit.
- BOSS TRex on Restored: beating the TRex gives the third SpellStone and opens the way back to Walled City. The
  prototype went back to the title with no stone.
- Third SpellStone on Restored: the Volcano Force Point now opens its second path and takes the third SpellStone,
  placing it gives the moonseeds Krystal plants in Moon Mountain Pass, and Walled City shows its Sun and Moon Stones
  once the third spirit is released. In the prototype both places stayed as on the first visit.
- Every restoration now has its own setting, and Original plays the prototype as it was left there: Tricky's Run is a
  new setting for Tricky's run to his mother in SwapStone Hollow, Cape Claw's shrine test is now under Krazoa
  Shrines, and that setting's Finished is now called Restored (a saved Finished reads as Restored). Crash fixes stay
  on with either value.
- Krazoa Shrines and Krazoa Tablets on Restored: the seqpoints that release Golden Plains' and Northern Wastes'
  spirits at Warlock Mountain, and Cape Claw's third tablet, no longer vanish while the player is still far from
  them, so each is there when the player arrives.
- Walled City on Restored: the Queen EarthWalker waits outside the city and gives Tricky his flame, a RedEye angered
  by it chases Sabre and drops a tooth when it catches him, ten teeth open the city's gate, and the King EarthWalker
  activates the third SpellStone. The prototype never built any of it.
